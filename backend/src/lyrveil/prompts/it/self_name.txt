# V6 (docs/consistency-engine.md, Corrected): the phrases with which the character names itself. A word that starts
# with a capital letter right after one of them, and is not a word of the character's own name or of [not_names], is a
# wrong name. The phrases ignore case and accents. A phrase right after a negation ("non sono Luca") does not count.
[phrases]
mi chiamo
il mio nome è
sono
chiamami

[not_names]
