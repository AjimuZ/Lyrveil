# V11 and V12 (docs/consistency-engine.md, Corrected and Recorded): Italian gender agreement in the first and in the
# second person. A hit is a lead of [first_person] (V11) or [second_person] (V12), at most one adverb of [adverbs], then
# a stem of [stems] ending in "o" (masculine) or "a" (feminine) that differs from the gender of the speaker the lead
# names: the card's `grammatical_gender` for V11, the user's for V12. Plural endings never count, and text inside
# quotation marks is skipped. Matching ignores case and accents and needs whole words. The list is closed on purpose:
# words that are also adverbs ("solo", "certo") are left out, because "sono solo stanca" must not count.
