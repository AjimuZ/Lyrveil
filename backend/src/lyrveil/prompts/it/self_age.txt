# V10 (docs/consistency-engine.md, Corrected): the phrases with which the character states its age. {n} stands for
# the age, in digits or, in Italian, in words ("trentun"); {end} for the end of a clause (punctuation or the end of the
# text). A stated age other than the card's is a hit. The phrases ignore case and accents. A word of [not_before] right
# after the phrase makes it a duration or a difference, so it does not count ("ho 3 anni di esperienza").
[phrases]
ho {n} anni
ho appena compiuto {n} anni

[not_before]
di
da
fa
più
meno
prima
dopo
in
per
